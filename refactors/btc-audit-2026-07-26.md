# BTC Signal Audit — 2026-07-26

**Scope:** `#btc-signals` + `#btc-recon` (BTC pipeline + BloFin execution). Poly/EW/BZ excluded.
**Mission:** how capable is this system at identifying *highly probable trade setups*?
**Data:** `trades.json` n=801 (768 confirmed+closed), 2026-04-13 → 2026-07-26 (104 days, 71 trading days);
`logs/zone-parity.jsonl` n=1968 cycles; Binance 30m klines n=5092 (independent ground truth).

---

## Verdict

**The system reliably finds levels price is near. It has not demonstrated positive expectancy after costs.**

The headline metric — 72% win rate, +1.27R per signal, +972R — is an artifact of a measurement
defect, not a description of tradeable performance. Re-walked against Binance ground truth:

| Accounting | n | win rate | avg R | day-clustered 95% CI on mean R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Canonical** (`trades.json`, what reports show) | 768 | 72.0% | **+1.265** | [+1.04, +1.50] |
| **Entry-gated** (limit order at recorded `entry`) | 670 | 39.0% | **+0.109** | [−0.11, +0.35] ← includes 0 |
| **Spot-fill** (market entry at signal price = what BloFin does) | 768 | 69.1% | **+0.224** | [+0.03, +0.46] |

Taker fees on the spot-fill book are **0.21R/signal (median), 0.44R (mean)** — the fee alone
consumes the gross edge. Net expectancy lands between **−0.22R and +0.02R per signal**:
statistically indistinguishable from zero, tilted negative.

This is independently corroborated by exchange truth already on file: the 2026-07-02 attribution
measured **−0.71R/signal** delta and a **−$47.74** strategy-attributable book
([2026-07-02-phase-d-attribution.md](2026-07-02-phase-d-attribution.md)). Two independent methods,
same conclusion.

## Tier 1 — Defects (provable from code / data alone; no tuning judgement required)

### D1 — Outcome walk credits TPs on positions that were never entered ★★★
`scripts/trigger-check.js:1870` `walkBarsForOutcome()` never references `t.entry`. It walks bars
from `firedAt` and books the full planned R at the first TP touched, regardless of whether price
ever traded at the entry.

The trigger buffer is `max(price × 0.005, zone_width × 1.5)`, so a signal fires while price is up
to 0.5% from the zone — but `entry` is set *at the zone*. Measured across 768 confirmed trades:

- **median gap at fire = 1.29R** — price is already 1.29R past the entry, favourably
- 683/768 (89%) fire with price already past entry; 456 (59%) by >1R; 205 (27%) by >2R
- **98 trades never traded at their entry price at all — canonical credited all 98 as wins, +274.4R** (28% of total book R)

The never-filled set is 100% winners *by construction*: price failing to return to entry is exactly
the case where it ran to TP. This is look-ahead bias, not a tuning parameter.

Worked example — `1784740836293-VAH-65723` (2026-07-22): fired at price 66027 with entry 65705
(0.49% below), stop 65562, tp1 66134. Recorded rr1 "3.0" is `(66134−65705)/143`. From the price
that actually existed, the trade offered 107 points of reward against 465 of risk. Booked **+3.3R**;
the executed track on the same signal says **stop**.

### D2 — Zone-parity P3 gate FAILS on all nine metrics ★★★
`node scripts/audit/zone-parity-report.js` → **exit 1**, 1968 samples over 14.2 days:

| metric | gate | measured |
|---|---|---|
| POC median / p95 | ≤0.10% / ≤0.25% | **1.985% / 6.430%** |
| VAH median / p95 | ≤0.10% / ≤0.25% | **0.424% / 1.387%** |
| VAL median / p95 | ≤0.10% / ≤0.25% | **0.419% / 3.094%** |
| trigger agreement | ≥95% | **34.3%** |
| CVD sign agreement | ≥99% | **64.2%** |
| OI trend agreement | ≥99% | **86.8%** |

**Diagnosis — the TV side moved, not the computed side.** Value-area width (VAH−VAL, % of price)
by day: TV swings **2.81% → 7.58%** (2.7×, with a 7.18/7.58/6.46 excursion on 07-15→17 then a
collapse to 3.05 on 07-18) while the frozen-window computation compresses smoothly 6.67 → 3.66.
Market structure cannot produce that discontinuity in one day; a changed chart viewport can.
Per the plan's own rule this triggers *"recalibrate + restart the 14-day clock."*

Per-cycle stability confirms neither side is noisy (median adjacent-cycle jump 0.000% for both) —
it is a persistent level offset that steps when the viewport steps.

### D3 — TV zone read unavailable in 14.6% of cycles ★★★
288 of 1968 parity cycles have no TV zone read (computed side: 0 failures). Observed live during
this audit at 16:10 UTC: `VRVP unavailable after 8 attempts: no data`. Roughly one poll in seven,
BTC signal generation is structurally blind — silently, as a no-trigger.

### D4 — `rr3` is uncapped and now contaminates the executed track ★★★
Stop distance is effectively constant at **0.216% of price** (p10 0.214%, p50 0.216%, p90 0.332%) —
a fixed fraction, not structural. TP3 is placed structurally. The ratio explodes:
`rr3` p50 3.4 · p90 15.1 · p95 18.6 · **max 88.6**. 140/768 plans (18%) carry rr3 > 10R.

`lib/executed-walk.js` is correct code, but pays `(rr1+rr2+rr3)/3` — so an 18.9R third rung yields
**executedPnlR 8.40R**. Current executed track: n=73, avg **+2.63R**, outcomes *only* `tp3` (44) or
`stop` (29), never tp1/tp2. This is the same phantom class healed on 2026-07-04, back again.
`rewalk-executed.js --apply` has not been re-run (memory flagged it as required before the D→E gate).

### D5 — Outcomes are stamped from in-progress bars, defeating the documented same-bar rule ★★
Re-walking with completed Binance bars reproduces only 646/768 (84.1%) of canonical outcomes.
The mismatch is directional: 92 cases where the completed bar reached a *higher* TP than booked
(tp1→tp2 43, tp1→tp3 25, tp2→tp3 24) — consistent with reading a still-forming 30M bar — and
**25 cases booked as wins that the completed bar scores as stops** (tp1→stop 19, tp2→stop 5,
tp3→stop 1). BACKTESTING.md and the code comment both state *"stop wins same-bar ambiguity —
conservative."* Evaluating incomplete bars silently inverts that guarantee.

### D6 — The confirmation filter is inert ★★★
BACKTESTING.md: *"`confirmed: true` on 50-75% of entries"* and *"If they're the same, the filter
isn't adding value and the entry criteria need review."*

Measured: **95.9% confirmed** (768/801). 41.4% confirm within 10 minutes; **35.2% carry
`confirmedAt` identical to `closedAt`** — confirmed and closed in the same instant. Median
fire→close is **0.48h**, and 66.5% close inside a single 30M bar.

This is mechanically caused by D1: confirmation is *"a 30M bar closed beyond entry"*, and price is
already beyond entry at fire in 89% of cases, so the first bar close confirms trivially. The filter
described as the strategy's true entry trigger is a no-op.

### D7 — Signal deduplication is absent ★★★
Grouping by same direction + zone mid ±0.15% + within 24h: 768 signals collapse to **317 distinct
setups** (mean 2.42 signals per setup). Worst cases: **2026-07-24 long VAL ×31 (+93.0R canonical)**,
05-06 long VAL ×13, 04-27 long VAL ×11, 07-13 long VAL ×10 (+28.0R canonical — the same trade,
identical entry 61662/stop 61527/risk 135.0, re-fired ten times over ten hours while price sat
2.6–4.7R above the entry).

Every performance statistic treats these as independent observations. Lag-1 autocorrelation of the
outcome sequence is **r = 0.347 [0.283, 0.408]** → **effective n ≈ 372**, not 768.

## Tier 2 — Open questions (require your intent; no fix proposed)

**Q1 — What is `entry` meant to be?** A resting limit at the zone (then D1's gating is the fix and
the honest track is 39% wr / +0.11R), or shorthand for "enter now at market" (then entry/stop/TP
must be recomputed off signal price, and the honest track is 69% wr / +0.22R)? Today it is
neither consistently, and BloFin executes the second while `trades.json` scores the first. Every
downstream number depends on this single answer.

**Q2 — Is the ~0.216%-of-price stop intended?** It is near-constant across all 768 signals,
which makes R a fixed-percentage unit rather than a structural risk measure, and is the direct
cause of D4's rr3 explosion.

**Q3 — Should one setup be allowed to re-fire 31 times in a day?** Cooldowns exist per zone per
session; the observed behaviour suggests they are not binding on VAL longs in a trend.

**Q4 — Should BloFin tier sizing stay linked to A/B/C while the ranking is inverted?**
See T3-b — `tierMult` currently sizes A-tier largest.

**Q5 — Are the parity gate's CVD/OI thresholds measuring comparable quantities?** TV CVD and
computed CVD differ in both magnitude and sign (−916 vs +83; +1430 vs +266), consistent with
different anchoring windows rather than a data error. A 99% sign-agreement gate may be testing a
definition mismatch. Worth verifying before treating 64.2% as a computed-side failure.

# Addendum — Entry semantics resolved, and the skill test

Added same day, after the audit above. **This addendum corrects the audit.**
Q1 ("what is `entry` meant to be?") is not an open question — the codebase
answers it unambiguously. My Tier-1 D1 model treated `entry` as a resting limit
order. That was the wrong model of intent, and the corrected accounting changes
the conclusion's shape (though not its sign).

## A1 — `entry` is a breakout trigger, not a limit. This is settled.

Three independent places in the code say the same thing:

| Where | What it says |
|---|---|
| `scripts/trigger-check.js:1011` | *"Wait for a 30-min candle to close above $entry before entering."* |
| `scripts/trigger-check.js:1667` | confirmation fires when `bar.close > t.entry` (long) |
| `scripts/trigger-check.js:893` | `entry = zone.lo + zoneWidth × 0.2` — 20% **into** the zone, i.e. a level price must reclaim |

So the intended trade is: signal fires → wait for a 30M close beyond `entry`
(within `CONFIRM_MAX_AGE_SEC` = 1h) → **enter at market at that close**. The
honest fill is `confirmedPrice`, which the system already records. The honest
risk unit is `|confirmedPrice − stop|`. The walk must start at the confirming
bar, not at `firedAt`.

**Do not pursue the limit-retest interpretation.** Separately tested below: it
is both contrary to intent and worse in the data.

## A2 — Design-intent accounting (n = 768)

| | value |
|---|---|
| hit rate | **73.2%** — Wilson [69.9, 76.2] |
| gross avg R | +0.170 — day-clustered CI [+0.013, +0.363] |
| round-trip taker fee | 0.273R mean (0.235R median) |
| **net avg R** | **−0.102** — day-clustered CI **[−0.250, +0.070]** |

**The win rate is real. The payoff is not.** 73% of trades win, but the mean win
is +0.600R while every loss is exactly −1.000R. That is a high-hit-rate,
negative-expectancy structure.

Realized R by rung: tp1 n=314 net +0.115R · tp2 n=142 net +0.362R · tp3 n=106
net +1.024R. The book is carried entirely by tp3; tp1 — 41% of all outcomes —
barely clears its own fee.

## A3 — Why: three geometry defects, one binding constraint

1. **The stop is inside the noise.** Stop distance is a near-constant 0.216% of
   price = **0.62× the 30m ATR**; it sits inside a single bar's range in
   **82.4%** of signals. Losses are noise-driven, not thesis-driven.
2. **Risk inflates 2.11× between plan and fill.** Median
   `|confirmedPrice − stop| ÷ |entry − stop|` = **2.11×** (p90 4.10×, max 17.5×).
   Every advertised rr1/rr2/rr3 is overstated by that factor.
3. **The target is often already behind the market.** At fire, price is beyond
   TP1 in **31.6%** of signals; at the confirming fill, **31.6%** too. Those
   positions open past their first target: booked as wins, worth ≈0R, full fee.

**Binding constraint — fee leverage.** Median notional ÷ risk = **283×**. A
0.12% round-trip taker fee therefore costs 0.27R per trade against a stop only
0.35% wide. This is a scalper's cost structure attached to a swing-trade hit rate.

### Breakeven arithmetic

To reach zero, exactly one of these must change:

| lever | required | current | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| mean win W | ≥ 0.739R | 0.600R | **+23%** |
| hit rate p | ≥ 79.6% | 73.2% | +6.4pp |
| fee F | ≤ 0.170R | 0.273R | **−38%** |

Fee sensitivity on the same cohort: taker/taker −0.253R → maker/taker −0.138R →
maker/maker −0.024R → zero-fee +0.091R. **Nearly the entire deficit is fees.**

## A4 — The skill test: is direction actually predicted?

The 73% hit rate could come from forecasting skill or from payoff asymmetry
(small target, moderate stop). Strip the asymmetry: place a **symmetric ±k×ATR
bet at 1:1 payoff** from the honest fill. Then hit rate *is* the edge, and 50%
means no directional information.

| geometry | n | hit rate | Wilson | mean R, day-clustered CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ±0.5×ATR | 768 | 45.3% | [41.8, 48.8] | [−0.21, +0.01] |
| ±1×ATR | 768 | 47.8% | [44.3, 51.3] | [−0.17, +0.08] |
| ±1.5×ATR | 768 | 53.4% | [49.8, 56.9] | [−0.05, +0.19] |
| ±2×ATR | 768 | 52.7% | [49.2, 56.2] | [−0.07, +0.19] |
| ±3×ATR | 761 | 53.6% | [50.1, 57.1] | [−0.06, +0.21] |

Random-direction null on the same bars and timestamps: 49.1% (±1×ATR),
50.1% (±2×ATR).

**Every cell's day-clustered bootstrap CI on mean R includes zero.** (Wilson
alone flags ±3×ATR, but Wilson assumes independence and we measured lag-1
r = 0.347 — the clustered test governs.)

> **No directional skill is detectable at symmetric geometry.** The 73% hit rate
> is a product of where the targets sit, not of forecasting. This is the single
> most important number in the audit.

## A5 — Refuted: filtering out "late" signals

My hypothesis was that signals firing far past the trigger were the damaged
ones. **The data says the opposite** — performance degrades monotonically as the
filter tightens:

| filter | n | net avg R |
|---|---|---|
| all | 768 | +0.063 |
| gap ≤ 3R | 708 | −0.088 |
| gap ≤ 2R | 563 | −0.192 |
| gap ≤ 1R | 312 | −0.253 |
| gap ≤ 0R (price still at the level) | 85 | **−0.416** |

Signals that fire with price already running are the ones that keep running.
**The gap is not a defect to filter — it is the momentum.** Recorded so this is
not re-proposed.

## A6 — No geometry in the grid clears costs

20 combinations of market-entry + k×ATR stop + m×R target, n=768 each. Best cell
(2×ATR stop, 3R target) = +0.097R net, CI [−0.194, +0.401]. **Zero cells have a
day-clustered CI excluding zero.** Net improves monotonically as stops widen —
but that is fee leverage falling, not edge appearing.

## A7 — Tier 3: A-tier may be directionally *inverted* (NOT actionable)

At ±1×ATR, A-tier "Full Confluence" hits **35.2%** vs 49.4% for the rest —
−14.2pp, mean-R CI [−0.508, −0.082] which excludes zero, n=91. Fisher
p = 1.36e-2 against a BH-FDR critical value of 0.0111 at q=0.10 across 9 cells.

**It misses the bar by a hair and is therefore not actionable.** But it is the
second independent measurement pointing the same way (the main audit found
A-tier had the highest win rate and the *lowest* expectancy), and A-tier
currently receives the **largest** BloFin position multiplier (1.0× vs C's 0.3×).
Re-test when n(A) ≥ 150. Do not tune on it now.

Under honest accounting no segment — tier, zone type, or direction — has a
day-clustered CI excluding zero. There is no profitable subset to retreat to.

## A9 — Revised recommendation

**The entry-semantics fix is a correctness fix, not a performance fix.** Adopting
design-intent accounting makes `trades.json` agree with BloFin and with the
2026-07-02 attribution verdict (−0.71R/signal). It will not make the system
profitable — it will make it *honestly* −0.10R/signal.

Ordered, with the gate each step must clear:

1. **Rewrite the outcome walk to design-intent semantics** — fill at
   `confirmedPrice`, R unit `|confirmedPrice − stop|`, walk from the confirming
   bar, unconfirmed ⇒ no trade, fees charged in R. Discard the existing
   baselines; they measure the artifact. *(No new data needed.)*
2. **Productionise the skill test** as `scripts/audit/skill-test.js` and make it
   the standing acceptance gate: any signal change must beat 50% on a symmetric
   ±k×ATR bet with a day-clustered CI excluding zero. Today's answer is **no**.
3. **Freeze BloFin Phase D→E.** The D→E gate compares paper expectancy against
   exchange truth; the paper side is currently a different strategy from the one
   being executed. No live capital while step 2 fails.
4. **Attack the cost structure, since it is the binding constraint** — the two
   levers with a mechanism are a structural ATR-based stop
   (`zone_edge ∓ max(0.002×price, 1.0×ATR30)`) and getting off taker fees. Both
   must be *measured*, not assumed: post-only entries reintroduce the fill
   problem the limit interpretation would have caused.
5. **Add an economic pre-trade gate** — refuse to fire when TP1 is already behind
   the market (31.6% of current signals) or when expected R after fees is below a
   floor. This is pure subtraction of known-negative trades.
6. **Retire the 85/74/63 probability constants** until they are re-derived
   out-of-sample. Under honest accounting the tiers do not separate, and A-tier
   may be inverted (A7). Publishing "85%" on a tier that nets −0.168R is the
   single most misleading output the system produces.

**Insufficient evidence** to recommend a specific stop width, target spacing, or
tier threshold. Steps 1–2 must land first; tuning before the ledger is honest and
the skill question is settled would be fitting to a metric we know is wrong.
