# Early-warning clues for big BTC moves - pre-registration (2026-11-10)

Written before any result was computed. The user (a novice learning to read BTC) asked for alerts
that give **at least 15 minutes of heads-up before a big move**. Direction is not the target here:
nearly 300 direction ideas have failed on this corpus. The question is narrower: which clues make a
big move, either way, more likely to arrive after a 15-minute warning, beyond what the market's
current volatility already says?

Prior: 2026-10-31 (1h bars, 2 years) found "volume ≥3x while calm" → ≥1.5% within 6h at 38% vs 28.5%
(1.32x), and that squeezes/quiet markets are *less* likely to break out. Big moves cluster: a market
that is already moving keeps moving (1.76x), so any clue correlated with recent volatility will look
good against the plain base rate. That is why the main comparison is volatility-matched.

## Data
- Binance BTCUSDT perpetual 5m candles with taker-buy volume (`.market-data-cache/ecl-5m.json`, to
  2026-11-09 21:10 UTC); 15m candles built from three 5m candles.
- 5-min open interest, data.binance.vision archive (`oi-archive-5m.json`, to 2026-11-08 23:55 UTC;
  gaps are unknown, never carried forward).
- 8-hourly funding history, Binance REST `/fapi/v1/fundingRate` (full depth).
- Order-book corpus `data/orderbook/*.ndjson` (1-minute rows, 2026-07-26 →). Known defects excluded
  as documented in notes/flow-research/orderbook-corpus-scoping-2026-09-06.md: `gap` rows, rows with
  `samples > 600` (double-counted sockets), liquidation columns before 2026-07-27T12:45Z,
  `liqAllLong/liqAllShort` (dimensionally invalid). The 2026-08-14 → 08-26 outage simply has no rows.

## Evaluation points and periods
Every 15m close T. In-sample 2021-01-01 → 2024-12-31, out-of-sample 2025-01-01 → data end.
Percentile thresholds are recomputed once per UTC day from the prior 30 days (prior 7 days for the
order-book clues, prior 90 days for funding), so no clue sees the future.

## Outcome (primary): a 1% move that arrives after the warning
From the alert price (the 15m close at T), the first 5m candle whose high ≥ +1% or low ≤ −1% falls
in **(T + 15 min, T + 2h15m]**, and no 5m candle touched ±1% inside the first 15 minutes. A move that
was already underway inside the warning window does not count: that is not a heads-up.
Secondary: (a) from the price at T+15m, a ±1% touch within the next 2h; (b) from the price at
T+15m, a ±1.5% touch within the next 6h (the 2026-10-31 definition).

## Clues (each fires at most once per 2 hours)
Long-history, eligible to pass:
- **E1 volume-while-calm** - 15m volume ≥ 3x the mean of the prior 20 candles, and the prior 6h range
  is not above the 90th percentile of 6h ranges (the 2026-10-31 rule at 15m resolution).
- **E2 OI build-up while flat** - OI change over the last 4h ≥ its 90th percentile, and the 4h price
  range ≤ its median ("bets piling up while price goes nowhere").
- **E3 funding extreme** - the last settled funding rate outside its 5th-95th percentile band.
- **E4 absorption** - 15m |taker delta| ≥ its 95th percentile while 15m |close − open| ≤ its median.
- **E5 US morning** - weekdays at 8:15 AM New York time (15 minutes before US data releases at 8:30
  and an hour before the stock open). A calendar clue: known in advance.

Order-book corpus, exploratory only (~95 days in one regime; can be a lead, never a pass):
- **B1 thin book** - mean resting depth (dBid + dAsk) over the 15m candle below its 10th percentile.
- **B2 liquidation burst** - BTC liquidations (liqLong + liqShort) in the 15m candle ≥ their 99th
  percentile and > 0.

Controls (not clues): **P0 placebo** - random 15m closes, same count as E1 (seeded); expected lift ≈ 1
on both comparisons. **C0 already moving** - prior 2h range ≥ its 90th percentile; expected lift > 1
vs the plain base and ≈ 1 vs the volatility-matched base, which is what proves the matching works.

## Comparisons
- **Plain base rate**: the outcome rate over all 15m closes in the same period.
- **Volatility-matched base (primary)**: every 15m close gets the decile of its prior 2h range
  (cut points from the prior 30 days). A clue's expected rate is the mean, over its alerts, of the
  base rate of each alert's decile in the same period. Lift = observed rate / expected rate.
- Day-clustered bootstrap (10,000 resamples of alert days) for the lift CI and a one-sided
  p(lift ≤ 1).

## Pass bar - all of these, per long-history clue
1. Out-of-sample volatility-matched lift ≥ 1.2 with its 95% CI above 1.0.
2. In-sample volatility-matched lift > 1.0.
3. Survives Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.10 across E1-E5 (one-sided bootstrap p).
4. At least 100 out-of-sample alerts.
Order-book clues: "lead" if both chronological halves show volatility-matched lift ≥ 1.2 with at
least 30 alerts each; otherwise nothing.

Also reported, not gating: plain-base lift, alerts per day, hit rate, median minutes from alert to
the move, share of all big-move episodes that got a warning (an episode starts at the first 15m
close whose primary outcome is true after one that was not; warned = an alert in the 2h before),
and the share of hits that went up (to show whether a clue says anything about direction).
