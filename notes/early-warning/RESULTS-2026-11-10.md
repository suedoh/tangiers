# Early-warning clues for big BTC moves - results (2026-11-10)

Pre-registration: [PREREG-2026-11-10.md](PREREG-2026-11-10.md) (written before any result).

## Verdict: one strong clue, one weaker one, the rest fail

Outcome: a ±1% move that arrives **after** a 15-minute warning and within about 2 hours.
Base rate: 34.9% of 15m closes in 2021-24, 20.8% in 2025-26.

| Clue | 2025-26 alerts | 2025-26 hit rate | vs equally calm moments (95% CI) | 2021-24 | Minutes to the move (median) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E5 US morning, 8:15 AM New York, weekdays** | 484 (0.7/day) | **53.9%** vs 20.8% | **2.79x** [2.60, 3.08] | 1.75x | 90 | **PASS** |
| **E2 OI build-up while flat** | 378 (0.6/day) | 21.2% vs 20.8% | **1.33x** [1.07, 1.54] | 1.13x | 80 | **PASS, but see below** |

"vs equally calm moments" = the hit rate divided by the rate at other times with the same 2-hour
volatility (the pre-registered primary comparison). The controls prove the matching works: an
"already moving" market looks 2.2x better than average but ≈1.0x once volatility is matched, and
random times score ≈1.0x.

Order-book clues (exploratory, ~95 days): B1 thin book 0.92x / 0.98x, nothing. B2 liquidation burst
1.34x (17 alerts) / 1.68x [1.20, 2.19] (32 alerts) - too few in the first half to count as a lead
under the pre-registered rule; worth re-testing as the corpus grows.

## The catch on E2 (post-hoc, not pre-registered)
Big moves cluster in the US morning. Re-checked after the run:
- E2 matched on New York hour as well as volatility: 1.08x (2021-24), 1.20x (2025-26) - weaker.
- E2 alerts **outside 7-11 AM New York**: 1.04x / **0.91x** - nothing.
- Its cut-points are not fragile (1.11-1.61x across 9 variants out-of-sample).
So OI piling up while price is flat adds a little **inside the US morning** and nothing elsewhere.
It is a refinement of E5, not a separate alarm. The old volume-while-calm rule also mostly reflected
time of day (hour-matched 0.89x / 1.05x).

## What this means
1. **There is a real heads-up, and it is the clock.** On weekdays, the 2 hours after 8:30 AM New
   York (US data releases, then the stock open) bring a ±1% move about **half the time** in
   2025-26, versus 1 in 5 for a random 15 minutes, with a median of 90 minutes after an 8:15 alert.
2. **None of them says which way.** Up-share of hits: 41-55% for every clue.
3. **Most big moves still come unannounced.** E5 warned of 14% of 2025-26 move episodes, E2 5%.
4. Funding extremes, absorption and a thin book do not warn of anything measurable.

## For the learner (plain English)
- The most reliable "something is about to happen" signal is the **US morning**: 8:30 AM New York
  is when US economic numbers come out, and 9:30 AM is when stocks open. BTC moves 1% or more in the
  2 hours after that about half the time, versus 1 in 5 at a random moment.
- If open bets are also piling up while price goes nowhere that morning, the odds go up a bit more.
- None of this tells you up or down. It tells you **when to pay attention**.
- Outside those hours, nothing we tested gives a reliable 15-minute warning.
