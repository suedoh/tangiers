# Textbook setups on the 1h, 15m and 5m charts - none has an edge

**Question (2026-11-11):** the novice asked for smaller-timeframe alerts of "highly probable" setups. Do the three
desk-trader setups the coach watches (rejection, fake break, retest at key levels) work on the 15m or 5m chart, and
does any filter a desk trader uses (trend, flow, the US morning, a volume spike, the 1h trend) make one work?

**Method:** `node scripts/research/setup-odds-test.js` (pre-registered in its header). BTCUSDT perp 2021-2026, the
same daily levels as the coach, entry at the close, stop 0.05% past the extreme, target the next level (R:R >= 1 after
fees), resolved on 5m candles stop-first, fees 0.16% round trip. 81 cells (3 timeframes x 3 setups x 9 filters).
In-sample 2021-24, out-of-sample 2025-26, BH-FDR q = 0.10 on the out-of-sample p-values. Full table:
[RESULTS-2026-11-11.txt](RESULTS-2026-11-11.txt); the coach reads `notes/journal/setup-odds.json`.

## Result

- **Survivors: 0 of 81.** No cell is positive after fees in both periods with a significant out-of-sample result.
- **"Probable" (+1R before -1R, lower bound above 55% in both periods): 0 of 81.** Every cell sits at 45-58%.
- **Before fees, the setups average about zero:** 1h -0.02R, 15m 0.00R, 5m +0.04R per trade. They call direction no
  better than a coin flip.
- **Fees are what sink them, and smaller charts pay more:** 0.35R a trade on the 1h, 0.60R on the 15m, 0.87R on the
  5m, because a 5m stop is only about as wide as the round-trip fee.
- A few cells are slightly positive before fees in both periods (e.g. 15m fake breaks, +0.06R / +0.04R), but far too
  little to cover even maker fees.

## What it means for the coach

- The word **ALERT** is reserved for a setup whose measured record is positive after fees. None qualifies, so every
  setup goes out as **PRACTICE** with its record.
- 15m setups are sent only at Grade A (with the 4h + daily trend and the candle's flow); Grade B is 97% of 15m setups
  and loses 0.5R+ a trade. 5m setups are not sent at all (-0.84R a trade).
- The smaller-timeframe help is the **SETUP ZONE** warning: price within 0.08% of the box's floor or ceiling, so the
  novice can open the 15m chart and watch who wins the line.

Consistent with every earlier test in this repo: the order-flow academic backtest (2026-09-06, 24 cells, none), the
desk-entry and checklist backtests (2026-11-09/10, fail), and the rebuild rounds (155 cells, none actionable).
